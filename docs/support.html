What MeetMemento is

MeetMemento is a private journaling app. There is no account and no sign-in. Your journal stays on this device. The optional AI companion uses Apple’s on-device models and requires a device with Apple Intelligence.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create a journal entry?

On the Journal screen, tap the new-entry button. Type, or use the microphone to transcribe. You can attach a photo from the camera or photo picker. Tap Save.

How do I talk to the companion?

From Journal, swipe left or tap the chat icon in the top-right. Ask a question about what you have written. Replies cite the entries they used — tap a citation to open the source. On devices without Apple Intelligence, journaling still works; the companion is unavailable.

Where is my data stored?

On this device only, in encrypted local storage. MeetMemento does not sync to a server, does not use a cloud account, and does not send your words to a third-party AI service. Device backups (iCloud or Finder) are controlled by your own device settings.

How do I protect the app?

During onboarding you can set an optional lock (Face ID or a PIN). You can skip it with “Not now” and change it later in Settings. If you forget the PIN, use the device-passcode fallback on the lock screen. Do not uninstall the app to “reset” a forgotten PIN — that deletes the journal on this device.

How do I export or delete my journal?

Settings → Export Your Journal shares your real entries as Markdown and JSON (sample entries are excluded). Settings → Delete Everything permanently removes journal files, chat, lock, and related keys on this device and returns you to first launch.

What are sample entries?

Settings → Load Sample Entries adds a fictional journal so you can try the companion. Remove Sample Entries reverses that and never touches your own writing.

Voice transcription is not working

iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and Speech Recognition → enable MeetMemento. Transcription is on-device only; some languages may be unavailable.

There is no account. How do I sign in?

You don’t. MeetMemento has no accounts. If you install on a new device, it starts empty — there is no cloud restore of journal content through the app.
