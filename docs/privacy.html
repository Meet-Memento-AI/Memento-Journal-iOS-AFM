Privacy Policy for MeetMemento

Last Updated: August 2026

Overview

MeetMemento ("we", "our", or "us") is a private journaling app. It is designed so that your journal stays on your device. MeetMemento has no user accounts and no backend server: it does not collect, transmit, or store your data on our servers or with third parties.

Information We Collect

We do not collect your personal information. Everything you create in the app stays on your device:

  • Journal content: your entries, titles, and personalization (such as a display name and journaling goals) are stored on your device only.
  • No account data: the app has no sign-in, so we do not collect an email address, name from a sign-in provider, or account identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking: the app does not track you across apps or websites and includes no third-party analytics SDKs.

How Your Data Is Stored

  • Your journal content is stored in encrypted local storage on your device.
  • There is no cloud copy and no cross-device sync; your data does not leave the device through MeetMemento.
  • An optional device PIN protects access to the app.
  • Standard device backups (for example iCloud or Finder backups) are controlled by your own device settings, not by MeetMemento.

AI-Powered Features

MeetMemento's chat and related AI features run on your device using Apple's on-device foundation models. When you chat, the app finds relevant entries and generates replies locally.

  • On-device processing: your messages and journal entries are processed on the device and are not sent to us or to any third-party AI service.
  • No third-party AI processors: MeetMemento does not send your content to any external AI provider.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information. Because your data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us, there is no personal data for us to share. MeetMemento uses no third-party data processors.

Your Rights and Control

  • Delete: you can delete individual entries in the app, or remove all app data by using the app's delete-everything option and/or uninstalling the app.
  • Access and export: your data lives on your device; export options may be offered within the app.
  • Because we hold no copy of your data, requests to access or delete data held by us do not apply — there is nothing stored on our side.

Children's Privacy

MeetMemento is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. The app does not transmit personal information off the device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy in the app or on our website. Your continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
