Privacy Policy for MeetMemento

Last Updated: September 2026

Overview

MeetMemento ("we", "our", or "us") is a private journaling app. It is designed so that your journal stays on your device. MeetMemento has no user accounts. Journal entries, chat history, and retrieval excerpts are stored on your device (and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud database). They are not synced to a Memento backend.

Information We Collect

We do not collect your journal. The only data that can leave the device for us is volunteered quality feedback, and only when you turn that on:

  • Journal content: your entries, titles, and personalization (such as a display name and journaling goals) are stored on your device. We do not upload them.
  • No account data: the app has no sign-in, so we do not collect an email address, name from a sign-in provider, or account identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking: the app does not track you across apps or websites and includes no third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Quality feedback (opt-in): if you enable Share quality feedback in Settings, thumbs, reason chips, and notes can be sent to our verification database (hosted on Supabase) so we can review answer quality. The question and answer text are sent only when you file a Report and choose to include them. Turning the setting off, or using Delete Everything, requests erasure of that device's feedback.

How Your Data Is Stored

  • Your journal content is stored in encrypted local storage on your device.
  • There is no Memento cloud copy of your journal and no journal sync through MeetMemento. Optional quality feedback, if you enable it, is stored in our verification database until you erase it.
  • An optional device PIN protects access to the app.
  • Standard device backups (for example iCloud or Finder backups) are controlled by your own device settings, not by MeetMemento.

AI-Powered Features

MeetMemento's chat and related AI features run on your device using Apple's on-device foundation models. When you chat, the app finds relevant entries and generates replies locally.

  • On-device processing: your messages and journal entries are processed on the device and are not sent to us or to any third-party AI service.
  • No third-party AI processors: MeetMemento does not send your content to any external AI provider.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information for advertising. Journal content is not transmitted to us. If you opt in to quality feedback, we use Supabase as a write-only processor for that volunteered feedback so we can review product quality. We do not read it back into the app.

Your Rights and Control

  • Delete: you can delete individual entries in the app, or remove all app data by using the app's delete-everything option and/or uninstalling the app.
  • Access and export: your data lives on your device; export options may be offered within the app.
  • Delete Everything in the app also requests deletion of any quality feedback that device sent. If you have questions about feedback we may still hold, contact us.

Children's Privacy

MeetMemento is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy in the app or on our website. Your continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
