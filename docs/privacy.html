Privacy Policy for Memento

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

Memento is a private journaling app for iPhone and iPad, developed by Sebastian Mendo ("we", "our", "us"). This policy explains exactly what happens to what you write. It is written to be specific rather than reassuring: where something does leave your device, we say so and say precisely what it is.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in. We do not know who you are.
  • Your journal is not uploaded to us. We have no server copy and no way to read your entries.
  • The AI companion runs on your device. Your entries are not sent to us or to any third-party AI service.
  • There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking. The app links no third-party code.
  • Three things do leave the device, and each is described below: a place name lookup (to Apple), an iCloud copy of your journal (to your iCloud account), and quality feedback you choose to send us.

1. What Memento stores, and where

Everything you create lives on your device:

  • Journal entries — title, text, and timestamps.
  • Photos attached to entries, stored encrypted on the device.
  • Voice capture. When you dictate, audio is transcribed to text. By default the audio file is discarded immediately after transcription.
  • Moods and topics inferred on the device from your entries.
  • A place name — a short display string such as "Dallas, TX" — when you choose to attach one. See section 4.
  • Chat conversations with the AI companion, and the reflections it produces.
  • Personalization — the name and the setup answers you gave during onboarding.
  • A search index of your entries, built on the device so the companion can find what you wrote.

We never receive any of it.

2. How it is protected on the device

  • Entry files and photos are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is generated on your device, stored in the iOS Keychain as device-only (it cannot be extracted to another device), and never leaves the device or reaches us.
  • App data files are written with iOS Data Protection, so they are unreadable while the device is locked.
  • You can set an optional app lock (Face ID, Touch ID, or a PIN). The PIN is held in the Keychain and is never uploaded. Because there is no account, there is no password-reset path — that is the trade-off for having no server.
  • Some data — your display name, your setup answers, your preferences, and the most recent weekly reflection — is stored in ordinary app preferences rather than in the encrypted store. It stays within the app's private container on the device and is covered by iOS Data Protection and your device passcode, but it is not separately encrypted by us.

3. iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Memento keeps a copy of your journal in your own private iCloud database so it can appear on your other Apple devices. To be exact:

  • This copy lives in your Apple account, under Apple's terms and Apple's encryption. It is not our infrastructure and we cannot read it, query it, or recover it for you.
  • What is mirrored: entry titles and text, moods, topics, place names, your chat conversations, reflections and their citations, and your personalization profile.
  • What is not mirrored: photo and audio files, the on-device search index, the read-aloud audio cache, your PIN, and your encryption key. Those stay on the device that created them.
  • Control: Memento has no in-app sync switch. You control this through iOS — sign out of iCloud, or turn off iCloud for Memento in iPhone Settings, and the mirroring stops.

4. Location

When you choose to attach a place to an entry, Memento takes a single, reduced-accuracy location reading and asks Apple's mapping service to turn it into a place name.

  • Your coordinates are sent to Apple as part of that lookup, the same way any app's place-name lookup works. Apple's handling of it is governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Your coordinates are never stored and never sent to us. Only the short text result — for example "Dallas, TX" — is saved with the entry.
  • It happens only when you tap to add a place while writing an entry. There is no background location tracking, and the app never asks for "Always" location access.
  • Deny or revoke the location permission in iPhone Settings and entries simply have no place name.

5. The AI companion

The companion answers questions about your own journal. It requires a device with Apple Intelligence, and it can be turned off entirely in Settings.

  • It runs on your device. Retrieval, search, transcription, image understanding, and reply generation all happen locally using Apple's on-device models. Your entries and messages are not sent to us, and not to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or any other AI provider. There is no third-party AI processor.
  • Apple Private Cloud Compute. The app is designed so that heavier requests could, in a future version, run on Apple's Private Cloud Compute — Apple infrastructure that is cryptographically attested and retains nothing after the request. That path is not enabled in the current version; all generation runs on your device today. If we enable it, we will update this policy before that version ships, and the app's Settings already contain an "On-Device Only" switch that keeps requests local.
  • Speech. Dictation uses Apple's speech models installed on your device. Installing those model assets is a download from Apple; the transcription itself runs locally.
  • Photos in chat are analysed on the device to produce a text description for the model. The image itself is not uploaded.

6. Spotlight

Memento can add your entries to your iPhone's own Spotlight search index so you can find them from the home screen. This is off by default and is a local system index — nothing is transmitted.

7. The only data we receive: quality feedback

The AI companion is imperfect, and the only way we can fix a bad answer is to see it. Two separate mechanisms exist, with different consent rules. Nothing here happens silently — both require you to act.

What you doWhat is sentConsent
Report an answer The question you asked and the reply the app gave, plus the reason you selected and any note you wrote (up to 280 characters), plus the technical fields below. Per-report. Tapping Report is the consent, so this sends even if the ratings switch is off.
Thumbs up / thumbs down The rating, the reason you selected, any note, and the technical fields below. No question or answer text. Only if you turn on Share quality feedback in Settings. It is off by default.

The technical fields sent with either are: an app-generated identifier for the message and session, the app version, which prompt version and model produced the reply, whether the reply was degraded, whether a safety card was shown, how many entries the reply cited (a number — never which entries), and timestamps.

What is never sent, in either case: your journal entries, your entry text, the identifiers of the entries that were cited, your chat history beyond the single reported exchange, your name, your setup answers, your photos, your audio, or your location.

Please note: because a Report includes the question and the reply, and because the companion quotes your journal in its replies, a Report can contain personal writing. Only report an answer if you are comfortable with us reading that exchange.

Who processes it, and how it is identified

  • Feedback is stored in a database hosted by Supabase, acting as our processor. It is write-only from the app: the app can add feedback and erase its own, and never reads any of it back.
  • It carries no account, email, name, or advertising identifier. The only identifier is a random UUID generated on your device the first time you send feedback. Its sole purpose is to let you erase what your device sent. It is not linked to you, and it is reset if you delete the app.
  • It is used only to diagnose and improve the quality and safety of the app's answers. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to build a profile of you.

How long we keep it, and how to erase it

We keep feedback until it is erased. You can erase everything your device has sent, at any time, in either of these ways:

  • Turn Share quality feedback off in Settings — this requests deletion of that device's feedback, including any reports.
  • Use Delete Everything in Settings.

If the device is offline at the time, the erase request is held and sent on the next launch. You can also email us and we will delete it manually.

8. What we do not do

  • No accounts. No email address, no sign-in, no user ID.
  • No analytics or crash SDKs. The app links no third-party libraries at all. Any crash reports you send are sent by iOS to Apple, under your own iOS settings, and we see only what Apple shows every developer — aggregate, anonymous crash traces.
  • No tracking. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not use App Tracking Transparency because there is nothing to ask you about.
  • No advertising, and no sale or sharing of personal information — including no "sale" or "sharing" as those terms are defined under California law.

10. Your rights and your controls

  • Access and portability. Settings → Export Your Journal writes your entries out as Markdown and JSON that you can keep or move elsewhere.
  • Deletion. Delete individual entries in the app, or use Settings → Delete Everything, which erases your journal, chat, reflections, the search index, the audio and read-aloud caches, your preferences and lock, destroys the encryption key, deletes the mirrored copy in your iCloud database, and requests erasure of any feedback that device sent. If iCloud is unreachable at that moment, the app tells you the iCloud part is pending and completes it later rather than failing silently.
  • Correction. You can edit any entry at any time. We hold no copy to correct.
  • Objection and withdrawal of consent. Turn off Share quality feedback, or turn off the AI companion entirely, at any time.

If you are in the EEA or the UK: our legal basis for processing the feedback you send us is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time as described above. Everything else stays on your device or in your own iCloud account, so we are not processing it. If you are in California, we do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Because we hold no identifiable record of you, a request to access "your data" will usually be answered by telling you that we hold nothing beyond feedback tied to a device identifier — which you can erase yourself, without contacting us.

11. Children

Memento is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us feedback, email us and we will delete it.

12. International transfers

If you send us quality feedback, it is stored on servers operated by our processor, Supabase, which may be located outside your country. Everything else remains on your device or in your own iCloud account.

13. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app's behaviour changes, and we will change the "Last Updated" date above. If a change means data that previously stayed on your device starts leaving it, we will say so clearly in the app before that version ships.
