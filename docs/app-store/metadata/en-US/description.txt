Memento is a journal that pays attention.

Speak or type an entry. Memento transcribes it, files it, and remembers it — so when you ask what you said six months ago, it can answer, in your own words, with the entries to prove it.

It is an archivist, not an advisor. It does not tell you what to do. It tells you what you said.


WHAT IT DOES

Capture — Speak an entry and watch it become text as you talk, or type it. There is no wrong length. A sentence counts.

Timeline — Every entry, in order, searchable. Your words stay legible and yours.

Ask — Put a question to your own archive. "When did I start feeling better about the move?" The companion answers from your entries and cites the ones it drew from, so you can always check its work against your own words.

Keep the conversation — Any exchange with the companion can be turned into a first-person journal entry, so a thought worked out in conversation isn't lost.

Export — Everything you have written, in Markdown and JSON, whenever you want, at no cost. Your words are yours. That is not a feature; it is the point.


HOW IT HANDLES YOUR WRITING

No account. No analytics. No third-party AI. Your words are processed on your iPhone with Apple's on-device models.

Entries are stored only on your device, encrypted at rest. There is no server holding your journal, because there is no account to attach one to. We could not read your entries if we wanted to — they are not where we are.

You can lock the app with Face ID or a passcode. You can turn the companion off entirely and use Memento as a plain, quiet journal.


WHAT IT WILL NOT DO

Memento does not give advice, make recommendations, or comment on how you seem to be doing. It is not a wellness product and it is not a substitute for talking to a person. It observes and it remembers. That is the whole design.


REQUIREMENTS

The companion uses Apple Intelligence and requires a compatible iPhone. Capture, transcription, timeline, search, and export work on every supported device.
