1. RECORDING

It shows launch, onboarding (including the optional app lock and how to
skip it), loading sample entries, a voice entry with the recording

Not applicable, so not shown: no registration, login or account-deletion

One auto-renewing subscription, Memento Pro, annual or monthly.
Journalling is free and unlimited — writing, reading, search and export
are never gated. Pro lifts the free tier's ten-messages-a-day companion
limit and unlocks Weekly reflections and Patterns. The paywall is reached
by sending ten messages in a day, or via Journal > Insights > Weekly.
Restore Purchases is on that screen and in Settings. Crisis messages are
never limited or charged.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE

A private journal that answers questions about what you wrote. Speak or
type an entry; it is transcribed, filed and indexed on the device. Later
you ask your archive a question and get an answer from your own entries,
citing the ones used. The problem it solves: journals go unread, and
search finds only the words you used.

For adults who journal and want it private. An archivist, not an advisor:
it reports what you wrote, never advising or diagnosing. Not a health
product.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS

No credentials, and no sign-in, which is why those fields are blank.
Onboarding offers an optional app lock; "Skip for now" bypasses it, and
the lock screen has a device-passcode fallback, so no PIN is needed.

The companion answers from entries and a fresh install has none:

  Journal > initials button top left (VoiceOver: "Menu") > Profile >
  Settings > "Load Sample Entries"

Fictional, reversible from the same row. Then ask via the message icon top
right or a left swipe; capture via the compose button, allowing Microphone
and Speech Recognition; export via Settings.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES

On device, Apple frameworks only: Foundation Models for all companion
text, Speech, NaturalLanguage, Vision, LocalAuthentication, Core Spotlight
(off by default), and a bundled Core ML voice, never downloaded.

Reaching Apple: MapKit reverse geocoding to name a place when asked; the
user's private CloudKit database, which we cannot access; Apple's speech
models on first use; and the App Store, which processes purchases.

Two non-Apple services. Supabase hosts a write-only database for
volunteered answer-quality feedback. RevenueCat reads subscription status
only, keyed to an anonymous identifier. Journal entries and chat history
go to neither. No auth provider, analytics or crash SDK, ads, or
third-party AI.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES

The app functions consistently across all regions: the binary is identical
everywhere, nothing reads the device's country or gates a feature by
territory, the interface is English only, and the App Store shows prices
in local currency. Two things vary by device rather than region: the
companion needs Apple Intelligence, and dictation needs Apple's on-device
speech model. Without either, the app says so and the rest still works.
