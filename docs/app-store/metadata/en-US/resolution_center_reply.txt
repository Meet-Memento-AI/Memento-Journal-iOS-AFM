2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE

Memento is a private journal that answers questions about what you wrote.
Speak or type an entry; it is transcribed, filed and indexed on the
device. Later you ask your archive a question and get an answer from your
own entries, citing the ones used. The problem it solves: journals go
unread, and search finds only the words you happened to use.

For adults who journal and want it private. An archivist, not an advisor:
it reports what you wrote, never advising, diagnosing or using clinical
language. Not a health or medical product.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS

No credentials, and no sign-in, which is why those fields are blank.
Onboarding offers an optional app lock; "Skip for now" bypasses it, and
if enabled the lock screen has a device-passcode fallback, so no PIN is
needed from us.

The companion answers from entries and a fresh install has none:

  Journal > initials button top left (VoiceOver: "Menu") > Profile >
  Settings > "Load Sample Entries"

Clearly fictional, reversible from the same row. Then ask via the message
icon top right or a left swipe; capture via the compose button, allowing
Microphone and Speech Recognition; export via Settings > "Export Your
Journal".

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES

On device, Apple frameworks only: Foundation Models for all companion
text, Speech, NaturalLanguage, Vision, LocalAuthentication, Core Spotlight
(off by default), and a bundled Core ML read-aloud voice, never
downloaded.

Reaching Apple: MapKit reverse geocoding to name a place when asked; the
user's own private CloudKit database, which we cannot access; and Apple's
speech models on first use.

One non-Apple service: Supabase, hosting a write-only database for
volunteered answer-quality feedback. A Report sends that question and
answer; thumbs ratings need the off-by-default setting "Share Quality
Feedback". Journal entries and chat history are not sent.

No authentication provider, payment processor, analytics or crash SDK,
advertising, third-party AI, or third-party Swift package.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES

The app functions consistently across all regions. The binary is identical
everywhere, nothing in it reads the device's country or gates a feature by
territory, and the interface is English only. Two things vary by device
rather than region: the companion needs Apple Intelligence, and dictation
needs Apple's on-device speech model for the device language. Without
either, the app says so and the rest still works.
