REVIEW NOTES — Memento 1.0

1. NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED. NO DEMO CREDENTIALS NEEDED.

Memento has no sign-in of any kind — no account system, no server, no login
screen. Every feature is reachable immediately after installing. The Sign-In
Information fields are blank for this reason, not by oversight.

Onboarding offers an OPTIONAL app lock (Face ID or 4-digit passcode). It is
skippable ("Not now") and off unless enabled. If you enable it and need to get
back in, use the device passcode fallback on the lock screen.


2. HOW TO REACH THE CORE EXPERIENCE

Memento reflects on entries, and a fresh install has none. To see it working:

  a. Complete onboarding (~20 seconds; skip the app lock).
  b. Swipe in from the left edge > Settings > Your Data > "Load Sample
     Entries" adds a fictional nine-month journal, so the reflection features
     have something to work from. Reversible from the same row.
  c. Weekly generates a reflection over them, citing the entries it drew from;
     tap a citation to jump to the source.
  d. Patterns shows recurring themes.
  e. Ask answers a typed question, e.g. "what have I been worried about?", from
     those entries, and shows its sources.

To test capture: tap record, grant microphone and speech recognition access,
speak for ten seconds, stop. The text appears in the timeline. A recording
indicator is visible the whole time the microphone is active.

A demo video of this flow is attached to this submission.


3. HOW DATA IS PROCESSED (Guidelines 5.1.1, 5.1.2)

No account, no analytics SDK, no third-party AI service.

Entries are stored on device in SwiftData and synced only through the user's own
iCloud private database, which we cannot read.

Text generation runs on device via the Foundation Models framework, or on
Apple's Private Cloud Compute for longer-context work. No user content is sent
to any non-Apple service. The app shows which of the two produced each piece of
generated text, where it is shown. Speech is transcribed with Apple's speech
recognition framework. The app makes no other network requests.

Our privacy policy describes all of this and is linked from Settings in the app
as well as from the product page.


4. PERMISSIONS

  Microphone          recording voice entries, only while actively recording
  Speech Recognition  turning those recordings into journal text
  Face ID             unlocking the optional app lock; LocalAuthentication
                      only, never used for identification

No location, photos, contacts, calendar, or tracking permission is requested,
and the app contains no tracking functionality.


5. AGE RATING AND CONTENT

A private, single-user journal. No content is shared with anyone, no messaging,
no social features, no in-app web browsing, no advertising.

Generated reflections describe what the user wrote. The app does not give
advice, make recommendations, diagnose, or offer treatment information, and is
not presented as a health or wellness product. Where an entry suggests possible
crisis, the app shows a static, region-appropriate list of support resources —
fixed text, never model-generated.
