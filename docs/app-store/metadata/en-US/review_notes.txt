REVIEW NOTES — Memento 1.0

1. NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED. NO DEMO CREDENTIALS NEEDED.

Memento has no sign-in of any kind — no account system, no server, no login
screen. Every feature is reachable immediately after installing. The Sign-In
Information fields are blank for this reason, not by oversight.

Onboarding offers an OPTIONAL app lock (Face ID or 4-digit PIN), skippable
via "Not now". If enabled, the lock screen offers a device-passcode fallback.
Do not look for a PIN in these notes; the fallback is the recovery path.


2. HOW TO REACH THE CORE EXPERIENCE

Memento's AI companion draws on what has been written, and a fresh install has
no entries yet. To see it working:

  a. Complete onboarding (~20 seconds; skip the app lock).
  b. On Journal, tap the initials button in the top-left (VoiceOver: "Menu")
     to open Profile, then open Settings. Tap "Load Sample Entries". This
     adds a fictional journal so the companion has something to draw from.
     Reversible from the same row; it never touches real entries.
  c. Return to Journal. Swipe left, or tap the chat icon in the top-right,
     and ask a question, e.g. "what have I been writing about?". The reply
     is grounded in those entries and cites the ones it drew from — tap a
     citation to see the source.

To test capture: tap the new-entry button, grant microphone and speech
recognition access, and speak. The text appears as you talk.

To test photos: in a new entry, add a photo from the camera or the picker.
Camera access is optional and only used to attach a picture to an entry.

To test export: Settings → "Export Your Journal" shares the user's entries
as Markdown and JSON files (sample entries are excluded from exports).

NOTE: The AI companion uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models and requires
a device with Apple Intelligence enabled. On other devices the app degrades
gracefully — capture, timeline, search, and export all still work.


3. HOW DATA IS PROCESSED (Guidelines 5.1.1, 5.1.2)

No account, no analytics SDK, no third-party AI service, no server of ours.

Entries are stored only on the device, encrypted at rest with a key held in
the device Keychain. There is no cloud sync of any kind in this version.

Text generation runs on device via Apple's Foundation Models framework. No
user content is sent to any non-Apple service. Speech is transcribed with
Apple's speech recognition framework with requiresOnDeviceRecognition set to
true, so audio is not sent off the device for transcription.

Our privacy policy describes all of this and is linked from Settings → About
in the app as well as from the product page.


4. PERMISSIONS

  Microphone          recording voice entries, only while actively recording
  Speech Recognition  turning those recordings into journal text (on-device
                      recognition required)
  Camera              optional photo attached to an entry
  Face ID             unlocking the optional app lock; LocalAuthentication
                      only, never used for identification

No location, contacts, calendar, or tracking permission is requested, and
the app contains no tracking functionality.


5. AGE RATING AND CONTENT

A private, single-user journal. No content sharing, messaging, social
features, in-app web browsing, or advertising.

The AI companion converses about what the user wrote. The app does not
diagnose, offer treatment information, or present itself as a medical
product. Where an entry suggests possible crisis, the companion shows a
static support-resource card rather than generating advice.


6. WHAT IS IN THIS VERSION

First release. Voice and text capture with on-device transcription; photos
on entries; searchable timeline; an AI companion that answers questions from
the user's own entries with citations; Markdown and JSON export; optional
Face ID or PIN lock.
