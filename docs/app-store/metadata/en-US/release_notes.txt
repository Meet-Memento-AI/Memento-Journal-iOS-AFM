First release.

Memento is a journal that remembers what you wrote — with a companion that answers questions from your own entries and cites them.

This version:

- Voice and text capture, with live on-device transcription
- Searchable timeline of every entry
- Ask, answering from your archive with citations back to source entries
- Turn a conversation into a first-person journal entry
- Markdown and JSON export, free and unrestricted
- Face ID or passcode lock, optional and skippable
- No account, no analytics, no third-party AI
