First release.

Memento is a journal that reads what you wrote and reflects it back — weekly reflections, long-run Patterns, and Ask, which answers questions from your own entries and cites them.

This version:

- Voice and text capture, with live on-device transcription
- Searchable timeline of every entry
- Weekly reflections with citations back to source entries
- Patterns across months
- Ask, answering from your archive
- Read-aloud playback for any reflection
- Markdown and JSON export, free and unrestricted
- Face ID or passcode lock, optional and skippable
- No account, no analytics, no third-party AI
